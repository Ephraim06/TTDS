Questions, answered

Before you apply

Can I study from outside Canada?+

Yes. TTSD’s online model is designed for global participation and has already connected learners across more than 23 countries.

Do I need to be a pastor to apply?+

No. The college serves pastors, ministry teams, emerging Christian leaders and lifelong learners who want rigorous faith-centred formation.

What subjects are available?+

The academic vision spans divinity, ministry, leadership, business management, law, development studies, education, politics, public administration and family studies. Contact admissions for current availability.

President’s message

Professor Tom Tirivangani

Education is an agent of change.

For centuries, education has been used as a tool for human development. No meaningful progress can occur in a society or community without some form of education.

Our vision is to produce pastors who are well versed in theology and ministry, leadership, management, development issues and law—and who understand politics and public administration—so they can become informed agents of change.

“There is no better time to think about packaging yourself as a minister of the Word. The time is now.”

We want a holistic approach to education. The graduate must be conversant not only with theology, but also with the functionality of society. If we are to win the world to Christ, we must understand the world we serve.

Professor Tom Tirivangani
Founder, President & Chancellor
